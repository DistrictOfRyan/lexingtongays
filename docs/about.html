About Lexington Gays

What We Do

Lexington Gays is your weekly LGBTQ+ event guide for Lexington, KY. Every week, we aggregate and curate the best queer events happening around the city so you never miss a thing.

Whether it's community gatherings, drag shows, art exhibitions, happy hours, or nightlife events, we bring it all together in one place so you can plan your week and stay connected with the Lexington LGBTQ+ community.

Our Sources

Here's where the weekly event list comes from. We check these sources and more:

  • Lexington Pride Center (PCSO) and the Lexington Pride Festival
  • The University of Kentucky Office of LGBTQ* Resources
  • PFLAG Central Kentucky, the Fairness Campaign, and Queer Kentucky
  • Imperial Court of Kentucky, Kentucky Black Pride, and Bluegrass Black Pride
  • AIDS Volunteers of Lexington (AVOL)
  • The Bar Complex and Crossings Lexington
  • Lexington Lesbian Coffee House, Lussi Brown Coffee Bar, and Third Street Stuff and Coffee
  • HotMess Sports, The League Lex, USGSN Lexington, OutLoud Sports, and Frontrunners Lexington
  • Lexington Public Library Pride programming and the VisitLEX LGBTQ guide
  • Local church and faith communities, including Bluegrass United Church of Christ and the Unitarian Universalist Church of Lexington
  • Community calendars on Eventbrite, Meetup, and QLIST

Know of an event source we're missing? Drop us a DM on Instagram and we'll add it to our radar.

Guides and Directory

Beyond the weekly list, we publish long-form guides to gay Lexington, including gay bars in Lexington, moving to Lexington as an LGBTQIA+ person, and the gay Lexington travel guide. Our directory of LGBTQ+ owned and friendly Lexington businesses covers bars, restaurants, churches, and resources.
