Nothing to do in Lexington?
Sounds like a straight person problem.

Lexington, Kentucky has a warmer, busier LGBTQ+ scene than most people expect. This page rounds up the queer events in Lexington each week, drag shows, bar nights, Pride events, and community meetups, refreshed weekly. The scene centers on longtime anchors like The Bar Complex and Crossings downtown, plus the Lexington Pride Center and welcoming spots across town.

Questions about queer Lexington

What LGBTQ+ events are happening in Lexington this week?

Lexington Gays publishes a free, updated-weekly list of LGBTQ+ events across Lexington, Kentucky, laid out day by day: drag shows, bar nights, Pride events, and community meetups. The current week is on the homepage at https://lexingtongays.com/.

Where are the gay bars in Lexington?

The Bar Complex (224 East Main St) is Kentucky's oldest gay bar, open since 1980, with multiple rooms and a dance floor. Crossings Lexington (117 N Limestone St) started as a leather bar in the 1980s and is open daily until 2:30am. A fuller guide to gay bars and nightlife in Lexington is at https://lexingtongays.com/blog/gay-bars-lexington.html.

Are there drag shows in Lexington?

Yes. The Bar Complex (224 East Main St) runs drag shows Thursday through Saturday, and Crossings Lexington books drag queens and go-go boys on a busy event calendar. Drag nights falling in the current week are listed by day at https://lexingtongays.com/.

Is there an LGBTQ+ community center in Lexington?

Yes. The Lexington Pride Center (389 Waller Avenue, Suite 100), run by PCSO, is Kentucky's oldest LGBTQ+ organization, founded in 1977 as GLSO. It runs a free monthly food bank, victim advocacy, support groups, a library, and education programs, and it produces the Lexington Pride Festival.

What LGBTQ+ organizations and resources are in Lexington?

Alongside the Lexington Pride Center, the University of Kentucky's Office of LGBTQ* Resources (Gatton Student Center) runs a public events calendar that is largely open to the wider community, not only students. AIDS Volunteers of Lexington (AVOL, 1824 Hill Rise Drive, Suite 100) provides housing, HIV testing, and supportive services for more than 400 low-income people living with HIV/AIDS in the Lexington area. The full directory of LGBTQ+ owned and friendly organizations is at https://lexingtongays.com/directory.html.

Is Lexington LGBTQ-friendly?

Lexington's queer scene centers on downtown, anchored by Kentucky's oldest gay bar (open since 1980) and Kentucky's oldest LGBTQ+ organization (founded in 1977). The annual Lexington Pride Festival draws around 50,000 people to more than 200 booths. If you are moving here, https://lexingtongays.com/blog/moving-to-lexington-lgbtq.html covers neighborhoods, safety, and finding community.
