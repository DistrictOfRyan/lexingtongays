THIS WEEK LEXINGTON GAYS

July 20 — July 26, 2026

Nothing to do in Lexington?
Sounds like a straight person problem.

Lexington, Kentucky has a warmer, busier LGBTQ+ scene than most people expect. This page rounds up every queer event in Lexington each week, drag shows, bar nights, Pride events, and community meetups, refreshed weekly. The scene centers on longtime anchors like The Bar Complex and Crossings downtown, plus the Lexington Pride Center and welcoming spots across town.
